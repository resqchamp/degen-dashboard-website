Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Degen Dashboard ("the App," "we," "us") is operated by 8888 Media LLC. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Session data you log — game type, location, buy-in/cash-out amounts, timestamps
  • Photos you capture of TITO tickets, credit meters, and W-2G forms, used to auto-fill session data
  • Email address, if you sign up for early access or create an account
  • Basic device and usage information to keep the App working correctly and to fix bugs

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App's core functionality — session tracking, scanning, and year-end export
  • To generate your personal tax-record exports
  • To communicate with you about the App (e.g., early access updates), if you've opted in
  • To improve the App and fix issues

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your session or financial data for advertising purposes.

Where Your Data Lives

By default, your session data and scanned images are stored locally on your device. If cloud backup/sync is enabled in a future version of the App, that data will be encrypted in transit and at rest, and you will be able to opt out or delete it at any time.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data, including your account and any information we hold about you, at any time by contacting us (below). We will honor deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe, except where we are legally required to retain certain records.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting or analytics tools) to operate the App. These providers are contractually required to protect your data and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Not tax, legal, or financial advice. Degen Dashboard is a personal record-keeping tool. Nothing in the App or this policy constitutes tax, legal, or financial advice. Consult a qualified professional for guidance specific to your situation.

Children's Privacy

Degen Dashboard is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under 21 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.

Contact
